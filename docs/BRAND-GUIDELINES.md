## §01 Brand Foundation

**Purpose:** "We are in the business of creating **stories from data**. But only
if there is one to tell."

**Positioning:** "APLYD is the **Chief AI Officer** for institutions — a single,
accountable partner that holds the full picture from data foundation to
institutional intelligence. Not a vendor. Not a consultant. A steward."

**Three pillars**

| | |
|---|---|
| Intelligence | "Continuous, embedded, accountable — built on data that reflects operational reality." |
| Integrity | "Honest before helpful. Responsible AI as a parallel operational track, not a disclaimer." |
| Independence | "Every engagement designed with its own conclusion — institutions own what we build." |

**We are** / **We are not**

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Accountable end-to-end | A technology vendor |
| Embedded in operational reality | A strategy firm with shelf reports |
| Built to work ourselves out of the role | A pilot factory |
| Honest about what's broken | A diplomatic consultancy |

**Audience:** "Senior decision-makers in institutions that must **act with
intelligence**."

| Segment | Tier | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Government & Ministry Leadership | PRIMARY | "Secretaries, Director-Generals, and senior officials accountable for citizen outcomes — and increasingly accountable for the evidence behind their decisions." |
| Development Institutions | PRIMARY | "Bilateral and multilateral leadership commissioning programs where intelligence — not just delivery — is the measure of success." |
| Foundations & Research | SECONDARY | "Program officers and research leads who fund or run institutional intelligence work and need partners who survive contact with reality." |

## §02 The Logo

"A wordmark anchored by a centered gold dot — intelligence at the core of the
system. Strong typography, deep navy, quiet authority."

### Primary mark

| | |
|---|---|
| Mark | Wordmark + descriptor |
| Endorsement | By Athena Infonomics |
| Dot | Centered, gold, fixed ratio |
| Use | Default for all surfaces |

### Three approved forms — "Use the most generous version the surface allows."

| Form | Guidelines' stated use |
|---|---|
| **Full Wordmark** (PRIMARY) | "Default. **Web**, decks, documents, signatures." |
| **Monogram** (Standalone Mark) | "**Favicons**, social avatars, watermarks, secondary marks." |
| **App Icon** (Rounded Container) | "Mobile app icons, OS surfaces. **Only on rounded-tile contexts.**" |

### Construction

- **Clear space:** "(X) equals the width of the letter 'A' in the wordmark, (Y)
  equals the height of two dots."
- **Minimum size:** "Digital: **80 px wide** minimum. Print: 25 mm wide minimum.
  **Below this, use the monogram.**"

### Colour states — "Maintain the gold dot in all colored states."

| State | Use |
|---|---|
| LIGHT — Navy on Light | "Default. Use on white, off-white, and pale tints." |
| DARK — White on Dark | "Use on navy, deep imagery, and high-contrast backgrounds." |
| NEUTRAL — On Neutral Backgrounds | "Acceptable on warm-neutral surfaces (stationery, print)." |

### Placement

"The mark prefers corners. Lead with the **top-left** in most communications."
- PREFERRED — **top-left**
- ACCEPTABLE — **bottom-left (dark)**

### Misuse — "Never modify the mark. The list below is not exhaustive — when in doubt, don't."

Do not stretch or distort · Do not change the color · Do not rotate the mark ·
Do not place on busy imagery without overlay · Do not add effects, shadows, or
outlines

## §03 The Palette

"Deep navy. Quiet gold. Disciplined neutrals. The palette is restrained on
purpose — the work is the headline."
"**Two colors carry the brand.** Use this discipline as a feature, not a constraint."

### Primary

| Smoke | `#C8C8C2` | — |
| Hair | `#E4E4DF` | — |
| Paper | `#F5F5F2` | — |
| Off-White | `#FAFAF7` | — |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | — |

**There is no red, purple, green or any other hue in the palette.** Anything
outside this table is off-brand.

### Ratios & Accessibility

> "One color dominates. One supports. One accents. **Earned, not equal.**"

**70% NEUTRALS · 25% NAVY · 5% GOLD**

> "Neutrals carry the room. Navy carries the institution. **Gold marks only the
> moments that matter — the dot in the mark, a key statistic, a single line of
> emphasis.**"

Practical reading: gold applied once per item across a row of cards, or along a
continuous surface, is decoration and breaks this rule. Gold on a single
statistic, one emphasised line, the logo dot, or an active/emphasis state is
compliant. See also §06's "Gold accent reserved for active or emphasis states"
and §07 Social's "Use one accent only. Never decorate."

## §04 The Type System

"A humanist sans-serif typeface. Open Source." — **Lato**, three roles:

| Weight | Role | Stated use |
|---|---|---|
| **Lato Black** | DISPLAY | "Used for headings and subheadings. Bold and friendly." |
| **Lato Regular** | BODY | "All running copy, and captions. Clean, humanist, unobtrusive." |
| **Lato Light** | HIGHLIGHT | "**Editorial headlines, pull-quotes, chapter openers.**" |

### Hierarchy — "A six-step scale. Use the highest level the surface earns."

| Step | Size / Weight | Specimen |
|---|---|---|
| DISPLAY | 36 / Black | "Make It Bold" |
| HEADLINE | 28 / Bold | "Lead Clearly" |
| SUBHEAD | 20 / Medium | "Support The Story" |
| BODY | 16 / Regular | "Body text carries the story's detail." |
| CAPTION | 12 / Light | "Captions add quiet supporting context." |
| SPEC | 10 / Light Italic | "USED FOR LEGAL, CREDITS & MICRO-COPY AND LABELS." |

### ⚠️ Two notes that matter for the web build

1. **The six-step scale tops out at 36px. The website hero is 64–96px** (§07) and
   is specified separately as **Lato Light**. These are different roles, not a
   contradiction: Black is the 36px DISPLAY step used for document/deck headings;
   Light is the large editorial hero. The guidelines practise this themselves —
   the cover, every chapter opener, the page-27 website mockup and the page-33
   closing line are all set in Lato Light.
2. **SUBHEAD specifies Medium (500)**, which this project does *not* load (only
   300 / 400 / 400-italic / 700 / 900, see `src/layouts/Base.astro`). Any
   `font-medium` silently falls back to Regular. Rendering that step literally
   requires adding `@fontsource/lato/500.css`.

## §05 Voice & Tone

"How we sound when we write. Considered, plain, honest before helpful. **The
register of a senior partner, not a vendor.**"

| # | Principle | |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Plain over performative.** | "We don't use words that obscure. If a sentence reads like consulting, rewrite it." |
| 02 | **Specific over sweeping.** | "Claims are anchored in named institutions, real geographies, measurable outcomes." |
| 03 | **Honest before helpful.** | "We say the uncomfortable thing in the first meeting. Diagnosis precedes prescription." |
| 04 | **Quiet, not bashful.** | "Restraint is a posture of confidence — not absence of opinion. We know what we think." |

**Worked examples**

| WRITE | NOT |
|---|---|
| "Your data infrastructure is fragmented." | "We see real opportunities to optimise your data journey." |
| "We will tell you before we sign." | "We pride ourselves on transparency." |

## §06 Imagery and Icons

"Landscapes, infrastructure, institutional moments. **No stock-photo gestures. No
abstract data swirls.**"

| Aspect | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | "Landscape, infrastructure, institutional scale. Mountains, public buildings, fieldwork — **not faces in conference rooms**." |
| **Light** | "Natural, atmospheric. Cool morning or evening light. Avoid harsh studio." |
| **Color** | "**Cool palette. Blues, slate, mist.** Warm light accents acceptable when natural." |
| **Framing** | "Wide, unhurried. The brand sits **within** the image, never against it." |
| **Icons** | "Line-weight icons inside circular containers. **1.25pt stroke.** **Gold accent reserved for active or emphasis states.**" |

## §07 In Application

### Website — the section that governs this build

> "**Restraint at scale.** Large type, generous whitespace, navy & gold restraint."

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| **Webgrid** | 12-column / **1440px max** / **80px outer margins** / generous vertical rhythm |
| **Hero Type** | **Lato Light, 64–96px**, line-height **1.05** |

Reference mockup: nav = About · Solutions · Insights · Careers; hero =
"Building trusted infrastructure for public impact." (set in Light); subhead =
"APLYD partners with institutions to design, build, and scale AI systems that
drive meaningful change."; CTA = navy button, "Explore Solutions →".

### Social

"Square and 4:5 templates. Restrained, recognisable, monogrammed." Three types at
1080 × 1080: QUOTE, STAT, IMAGE.

> "Always carry the mark. **Use one accent only. Never decorate. Never overcrowd.**"

### Stationery

"Business cards and letterhead. Minimal, considered, on coated stock." Card front
= navy with white wordmark; back = white with navy type. Letterhead footer rule in
gold above `APLYD / CHN · LDN · WSH · NBO`.

### Decks

"Three slide types cover most needs." All decks: **16:9 widescreen, navy chapter
openers, white content slides, mono footer with page numbers.** Types: Cover Page ·
Section Breaker · Headings/content.

### Email signature

| Role | Lato Italic, 11pt |
| Contact | Lato Regular, 10pt |
| Logo | Right-aligned, **150px wide** |
